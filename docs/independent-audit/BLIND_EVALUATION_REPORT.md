## 1. Cold Discovery Log (What I Found on My Own)

### What the Product Does (Discovered Without Documentation)
Approaching the application cold at `1440×900`, **ContextPane** is a **multi-tenant MSP L2/L3 incident triage and remediation workbench** for Microsoft 365, Entra ID, Intune, and Azure (AVD/NSG/Policy). Instead of forcing an on-call engineer to bounce between PSA tickets, Entra admin center, Intune device configurations, and Azure network blades, it pairs:
- **Left Pane (`440px`):** The incoming PSA ticket (`#INC-4092`, `#INC-4099`), an AI-synthesized **Summary & Resolution Plan** with numbered cross-domain root causes, interactive entity chips embedded inside the ticket prose, collapsible **Contract/SLA (`#CNT-2026-884`)** and **Incident Timeline** drawers, and a screenshot attachment analyzer (`Extract Telemetry via Muse Vision`).
- **Right Pane (`1000px`):** A dynamically assembled stack of domain-specific **Context Cards** (e.g., *License Pool & Assignment*, *Sign-In History & Conditional Access*, *AVD Host Pool, NSG & Azure Policy*, *Intune Policy Conflict & Device Compliance*) alongside a **Time-Travel Scrubber** (`T-18m` baseline → `T-0` live incident → `T+Fix` projected convergence) and a human-in-the-loop **Action Approval (`PATH A` manual scripts vs. `PATH B` portal execution / GitOps PR)** drawer.

### Interactions That Delighted Me During Cold Exploration
1. **Inline Ticket Entity Chips & Hover Quick-Triage (`shot-01.png`, `shot-03.png`):** Turning user UPNs (`lynne.robins.contoso@...`), security groups (`SG-Contoso-Finance-AVD`), SKUs (`SPE_E5`), error codes (`AADSTS53003`), and Azure resources (`nsg-contoso-avd-hostpool-01`) directly inside the customer's prose into color-coded interactive pills immediately grounded the ticket in live tenant telemetry. Hovering a chip surfaces Microsoft Learn documentation for `AADSTS53003` and 1-click jump/stage actions (`Inspect Blocking CA Policy`, `Stage License Fix`).
2. **Selection-to-UI Context Spawning (`shot-07.png`):** Highlighting arbitrary prose inside the customer ticket (`"their Microsoft 365 E5 upgrade was approved on Monday"`) pops up a floating `✨ Generate Context UI` pill that synthesizes a bespoke 4th context card at the top of the right pane (`MATCHED: 3 users`, `DRIFTED / ERROR: 3`, `STILL ON SPE_E3: 3`) and leaves behind a persistent cyan provenance chip inside the ticket text.
3. **3-Way Commercial Reconciler (`shot-04.png`):** Comparing **PSA Contract (`12 seats`)** vs. **CSP Pax8 Billed (`12 seats`)** vs. **Entra Active (`9 seats`)** directly inside the license card (`3 paid seats sitting idle ($171/mo waste) · Zero new procurement needed`) solves a real daily MSP pain point—preventing L2 engineers from buying duplicate CSP seats when unassigned pool licenses already exist.
4. **Screenshot-to-Log Correlation via Muse Vision (`shot-05.png`, `shot-06.png`):** Clicking `Extract Telemetry via Muse Vision` on the customer's attached `AADSTS53003` screenshot extracts the correlation ID and timestamp (`matched sgn-contoso-0003 · Δ 0 s`) and highlights the exact matching sign-in row in the right-pane table with a `vision match` badge.
5. **Canary Ring-0 & Dead-Man's Switch Rollback Simulation (`shot-09.png`):** Providing an explicit `Simulate Canary Probe Failure (Test Dead-Man's Switch)` button inside the approval drawer that walks through `APPLIED → CANARY_RING_0 → SYNTHETIC_PROBED → AUTO_ROLLED_BACK` (`f⁻¹ restored`) directly addresses an MSP engineer's fear of locking out a tenant via a bad Conditional Access or NSG push.
6. **Intune Registry Tattoo Callouts (`shot-10.png`):** Explicitly flagging `tattooed on unassign` next to `HKLM\SOFTWARE\Policies\Microsoft\FVE\EncryptionMethodWithXtsOs` in `#INC-4099` shows genuine domain awareness of how legacy Intune OMA-URI / registry settings behave when unassigned.

### Features That Were Poorly Signposted, Hidden, or Confusing Without a Manual
1. **Hidden Tabs in the `Action Approval` Drawer (`shot-08.png`, `shot-09.png`):** The approval drawer crams 8 to 9 tabs into a `600px` horizontal bar with `overflow-x-auto` and **no scroll arrows, fade gradient, or dropdown overflow menu**. At `1440×900`, the 6th tab (`Inverse Rollback (f⁻¹)`) is sliced in half at the right edge of the screen, and the 7th and 8th tabs (`3-Way Commercial` and `Fleet & Reverse-IaC`) are completely invisible unless the user accidentally side-scrolls the tab strip.
2. **Highlight-to-Generate (`✨ Generate Context UI`) Has Zero Resting Affordance (`shot-01.png`, `shot-07.png`):** Nothing on the left pane hints that selecting plain English text in the ticket body will spawn a new telemetry card. I only discovered it because I intentionally tested text selection across the ticket body.
3. **1-Click Multi-Tenant Fleet Campaign Fires Immediately Without Scope Preview (`shot-10.png`):** Clicking `Stage Fleet GitOps Campaign (3 Tenants)` on an NSG or Intune card looks like it should open a preview modal showing *which* client tenants are affected. Instead, it immediately mutates state and stages Git branches across `contoso-finance`, `northwind-traders`, and `tailspin-aerospace`, notifying the operator only afterward via a transient bottom-left toast.
4. **Top-Bar `Router Savings` Pill Opens Inside a Drawer Tab Instead of a Popover:** Clicking `Router Savings · 0% 0-tok` in the top navigation bar opens the full right-side `System & Audit` slide-over drawer, whereas `Voice Steer` right next to it opens a persistent floating popover that refuses to close when clicking elsewhere.
5. **Ambiguous Color Coding on Left-Pane Summary Steps (`shot-01.png` vs. `shot-12.png`):** In `#INC-4092`, the three numbered circles in `SUMMARY & RESOLUTION PLAN` are colored purple (`1`), amber (`2`), and blue (`3`), which initially looks like severity coding or domain coding. When triaging an unscripted ticket (`#CUSTOM-ALJ4LA` in `shot-12.png`), all three steps render in red (`1`, `2`, `3`) despite being sequential remediation steps.

## 3. Part B — Post-Screenshot Text & Technical Audit

After locking in the visual findings from `shot-01.png`–`shot-12.png`, I inspected the HTTP payloads from `http://localhost:4000`, the persisted state files in [`data/`](file:///c:/dev/contextpane/data/audit-log.json), the generated remediation artifacts in [`data/remediations/`](file:///c:/dev/contextpane/data/remediations/rem-mvqtl6h2-tygq.json), and the frontend/backend implementation in [`apps/`](file:///c:/dev/contextpane/apps/orchestrator/src/app.ts).

### 3.1 English Summaries, Diagnostics & Resolution Notes
- **Internal Debug Telemetry Leaked into Customer-Facing PSA Note (`#CUSTOM-ALJ4LA`):**
  In [`psa-note.ts`](file:///c:/dev/contextpane/apps/orchestrator/src/local/psa-note.ts#L65-L84), [`buildResolutionNote`](file:///c:/dev/contextpane/apps/orchestrator/src/local/psa-note.ts#L65-L120) sets `const rootCause = snapshotBrief.summary` and interpolates `${rootCause}` directly into `customerSummary` (`FOR THE CUSTOMER — Ready to send via email / PSA portal`). For unscripted tickets, `snapshotBrief.summary` is an internal debug string, producing a customer email that starts with:
  > *"Hi team, Your issue is resolved. Unscripted ticket triaged live against c4a8shili.onmicrosoft.com: 6 entities matched MCP data (workload-identity, secret, oauth), inheriting the ANOM-APP-401 pattern..."*
  No MSP engineer could ever paste that into a client-facing ServiceNow or HaloPSA resolution note.
- **Misleading Post-Resolution Subtitle on Workload Identity Incidents (`#CUSTOM-ALJ4LA`):**
  When triaging the unscripted ERP app registration ticket (`AADSTS7000222`, expired secret, shadow-admin contractor owner, over-privileged `Directory.ReadWrite.All`), [`custom-scenarios.json`](file:///c:/dev/contextpane/data/custom-scenarios.json#L278-L280) only maps `"components": ["EntraSignInTimeline"]`. Once resolved, the card header displays:
  > *"Sign-In History & Conditional Access — Resolved · Conditional Access matches the golden baseline"*
  Conditional Access had nothing to do with `#CUSTOM-ALJ4LA`.

### 3.2 PowerShell, Microsoft Graph JSON & Terraform/HCL Audit
Would a senior M365 / Entra / Intune / Azure engineer trust and run the generated code in production? **Not without manual fixes.** Several critical domain bugs exist in the generated artifacts:

1. **`INC-4092` Group-Based Licensing Bug (`Set-MgUserLicense` on `PATH = Group` Users):**
   - **What the UI shows:** Card 1 (`License Pool & Assignment`) explicitly shows that all 5 users in `SG-Contoso-Finance-AVD` receive their licenses via **`PATH = Group`** (`SG-Contoso-Finance-Legacy` assigning `SPE_E3`, and `SG-Contoso-Finance-AVD` assigning `SPE_E5`).
   - **What the generated PowerShell & Graph JSON do ([`rem-mvqtl6h2-tygq.json`](file:///c:/dev/contextpane/data/remediations/rem-mvqtl6h2-tygq.json#L8-L55)):** `Invoke-Remediate-INC-4092.ps1` loops over each user UPN and calls `Set-MgUserLicense -UserId $upn -AddLicenses @(@{ SkuId = $e5SkuId }) -RemoveLicenses $remove` (and `POST /v1.0/users/{upn}/assignLicense`).
   - **Why this fails in real Entra ID:** Microsoft Graph **rejects** removing a group-inherited license (`SPE_E3`) via `assignLicense` with HTTP `400 Bad Request` (`LicenseAssignmentCannotBeModified: License assignment cannot be modified for user with group-based licensing`). Furthermore, calling `Set-MgUserLicense -AddLicenses` stamps a redundant **Direct** license path onto the user instead of removing the users from `SG-Contoso-Finance-Legacy` (`Remove-MgGroupMemberByRef`) and triggering `Invoke-MgLicenseUser` (`POST /users/{id}/reprocessLicenseAssignment`).
2. **Invalid Placeholders, Non-GUID Slugs & Destructive `PATCH` Bodies in `graphPatch` JSON:**
   - **Non-GUID group slug & overwritten `includeGroups` in `INC-4092` ([`rem-mvqtl6h2-tygq.json`](file:///c:/dev/contextpane/data/remediations/rem-mvqtl6h2-tygq.json#L58-L71)):** The `PATCH /v1.0/identity/conditionalAccess/policies/ca-contoso-avd-legacy-block` body sends `"excludeGroups": ["sg-contoso-finance-avd-01"]` (a human slug, not an Entra GUID) and passes `"conditions": { "users": { "excludeGroups": [...] } }` without preserving the existing `includeGroups` array—which in Microsoft Graph overwrites the `users` condition object.
   - **Unresolved `<... id>` placeholders across `INC-4099`, `ANOM-CA-204`, `ANOM-INT-309`, and `ANOM-LIC-101`:** Inspecting [`rem-mvpwbfpd-u1kj.json`](file:///c:/dev/contextpane/data/remediations/rem-mvpwbfpd-u1kj.json#L38-L52) (`INC-4099`), [`rem-mvpusogz-hryr.json`](file:///c:/dev/contextpane/data/remediations/rem-mvpusogz-hryr.json#L28-L35) (`ANOM-CA-204`), [`rem-mvptn5d1-v88l.json`](file:///c:/dev/contextpane/data/remediations/rem-mvptn5d1-v88l.json#L28-L35) (`ANOM-INT-309`), and [`rem-mvptibfc-br1m.json`](file:///c:/dev/contextpane/data/remediations/rem-mvptibfc-br1m.json#L28-L35) (`ANOM-LIC-101`) reveals literal angle-bracket placeholders in the `Copy Graph JSON` payload:
     - `"/deviceManagement/deviceConfigurations/<Fabrikam-Legacy-BitLocker-DeviceConfig id>/assign"`
     - `"/deviceManagement/managedDevices/<FABRIKAM-LT-1107 id>/syncDevice"`
     - `"excludeGroups": ["<SG-BreakGlass-Accounts id>"]`
     - `"skuId": "<SPE_E5 skuId>"`
     Yet the approval drawer badges these payloads as **`Graph schema check ✓`**!
3. **`INC-4099` BitLocker Registry Tattoo Gap (`PATH A` PowerShell vs. Hidden Tab):**
   - Card 1 in `#INC-4099` (`shot-10.png`) warns that unassigning the legacy OMA-URI profile leaves `EncryptionMethodWithXtsOs` (`AES-128`) **tattooed in the Windows registry**.
   - However, the primary `Invoke-Remediate-INC-4099.ps1` script in `PATH A` ([`rem-mvpwbfpd-u1kj.json`](file:///c:/dev/contextpane/data/remediations/rem-mvpwbfpd-u1kj.json#L8-L37)) **only unassigns the legacy profile and triggers `Sync-MgDeviceManagementManagedDevice`**—it never clears the tattooed registry keys or deploys the Intune Proactive Remediation (`deviceHealthScripts`). Those registry-cleanup scripts sit isolated in the `Detect.ps1 / Remediate.ps1` tab and are never pushed to Intune when approving via `PATH B`.
4. **`ANOM-APP-401` / `#CUSTOM-ALJ4LA` Destructive App-Role Wipe & Incomplete `Sites.Selected` Grant ([`rem-mvqu6p19-baqd.json`](file:///c:/dev/contextpane/data/remediations/rem-mvqu6p19-baqd.json#L8-L45)):**
   - **Wipes all app roles across all APIs:** `Invoke-Remediate-ANOM-APP-401.ps1` runs `Get-MgServicePrincipalAppRoleAssignment -ServicePrincipalId $sp.Id -All` and deletes **every** assignment in a `foreach` loop instead of filtering to the over-privileged `Directory.ReadWrite.All`, `Mail.ReadWrite`, and `Sites.FullControl.All` roles.
   - **Broken `Sites.Selected` implementation:** It grants the `Sites.Selected` app role (`883ea226-0bf2-4a8f-9f9d-92c9162a727d`) on Microsoft Graph, **but never calls `New-MgSitePermission` (`POST /sites/{siteId}/permissions`)** to grant the service principal access to the target SharePoint site. In M365, granting `Sites.Selected` in Entra without a matching SharePoint site permission leaves the ERP sync app with zero access to any site.
   - **Malformed Unified Diff Header:** The `terraformDiff` in [`rem-mvqu6p19-baqd.json`](file:///c:/dev/contextpane/data/remediations/rem-mvqu6p19-baqd.json#L81-L96) uses `@@ workload identity federation @@` instead of valid `@@ -l,s +l,s @@` line numbers (so `git apply` fails) and writes an Entra App Registration patch to the hardcoded path `demo-iac/remediations/CUSTOM-ALJ4LA/nsg-drift.patch` ([`app.ts`](file:///c:/dev/contextpane/apps/orchestrator/src/app.ts#L597-L601)).

### 3.3 Backend-to-UI State Honesty & Verification Integrity
1. **Partial Execution in `INC-4092` Live Approval ([`lab.ts`](file:///c:/dev/contextpane/apps/orchestrator/src/local/lab.ts#L39-L53)):**
   When `INC-4092` is approved in `Emergency Break-Glass` mode, the UI reports that all 3 domains converged (`SPE_E5`, Conditional Access policy `ca-contoso-avd-legacy-block`, and `nsg-contoso-avd-hostpool-01`). ### 1. Design (`7.6 / 10`)
- **What Works:** The visual hierarchy on landing (`shot-01.png`) is immediately legible. The slate/obsidian palette (`#0B0F17` / `#111827`), monospace telemetry badges, and semantic color tokens (crimson for `critical` / `AADSTS53003`, amber for `warning` / `REPORT-ONLY DRIFT`, emerald for `Compliant` / `RESOLVED & VERIFIED ✓`, purple for `vision match`) give the interface the density and authority of a serious NOC/SOC tool.
- **Why It Loses Points:**
  1. **Vertical Viewport Compression in `Action Approval` (`shot-08.png`, `shot-09.png`):** On a standard `1440×900` laptop display, dedicating ~680–710px of vertical height to fixed headers and footers leaves only **`110px–140px`** for the actual code/proof inspection pane.
  2. **Clipped & Overflowing Controls (`shot-08.png`, `shot-10.png`, `shot-11.png`):** The 6th tab in `Action Approval` is sliced in half at the right edge of the screen; the `Stage Fleet GitOps Campaign (2 Tenants)` button in `#INC-4099` wraps onto two lines inside a fixed `h-6` (`24px`) pill so its border cuts through the word `Conflict`; quick-sample pills in `Triage Unscripted Ticket` clip mid-letter (`...connect to AV`, `...check-in v`); and registry path strings clip mid-word (`...AllowWarningForOtherDiskEncrypti`).

### 2. UX (`8.1 / 10`)
- **What Works:** The end-to-end operator journey is deeply empathetic to how MSP engineers actually work. Bridging **PSA Contract (`#CNT-2026-884`) → Pax8 CSP Billing → Entra Active Seats** in a single view (`shot-04.png`) eliminates unnecessary license purchases. Offering **Path A (copy `-WhatIf` verified PowerShell/Graph/Terraform)** alongside **Path B (Approve in Portal: Break-Glass vs. Pure GitOps)** (`shot-08.png`) respects varying client change-control maturity levels.
- **Why It Loses Points:**
  1. **Trust-Eroding Time-Travel Contradictions (`shot-06.png`):** Scrubbing to `T-18m (Healthy Baseline)` tells the operator `0 AADSTS53003 blocks` and `Compliant` in the headers while still displaying the 3 red `AADSTS53003` blocks and the rogue `Allow-Temp-RDP-Diagnostic-Drift` rule in the tables directly underneath.
  2. **Zero-Confirmation Multi-Tenant Mutation:** Clicking `Stage Fleet GitOps Campaign (3 Tenants)` immediately stages changes across three client tenants without a scope preview or confirmation modal.

- **What Works:** Triage speed is outstanding. Starting with collapsed context cards that expose 1-line root-cause KPIs (`shot-01.png`) prevents scroll fatigue. ### 4. Technical Standpoint (`6.8 / 10`)
